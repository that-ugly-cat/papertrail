PaperTrail

From idea to published paper

Where every manuscript stands, from the idea to the version of record. Not a list of things to do: a record of where each one is, and how long it has been sitting there.

Projects, and the state they are actually in

Workspaces with per-project membership, co-authors, links, notes. Every project has a status, and every status has a date.

Submissions and outcomes

Which venue, when, how it went. Open submissions stay in sight until they have an answer, which is the only way not to lose one.

The number that matters

How long a thing has been still. A tracker that only says «in progress» is no use: what gets forgotten is not what you are working on, it is what you stopped working on without deciding to.

For an assistant too

An MCP surface with its own revocable key, so «what is stuck?» can be answered without anyone having to say it first.
